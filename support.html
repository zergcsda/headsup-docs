Common questions

Tilting doesn’t register. What can I do?

Hold the phone sideways at your forehead and keep it steady for a moment when the round starts, so it can calibrate. Between cards, return the phone to the center before tilting again. If tilting still feels unreliable, tap Play with buttons and use the on-screen buttons to mark Correct or Skipped.

How do I make a custom deck?

Go to the My decks tab and tap Make a deck. Give it a name and enter one word per line. Your deck is saved on your iPhone.

How do I delete a custom deck?

In the My decks tab, tap the trash icon on the deck you want to remove.

How do I change the language?

Good Company follows your device language. To choose a different one, open Settings > Language and pick English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Open Settings and switch off Sound effects or Haptic feedback.

My round paused when I left the app.

That’s on purpose, so you don’t lose time. When you come back, tap Continue round to keep playing or Finish round to see your recap.

Do I need to restore a purchase?

No. Good Company is a paid download with no in-app purchases. On a new iPhone, download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account at no extra cost.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
