The App

headsup is a guessing game you play together in person. It includes:

  • Rounds of 30, 60 or 90 seconds with a 3-2-1 countdown
  • Tilt controls (down for correct, up to skip) and an on-screen button alternative
  • 8 built-in decks of 40 cards each, plus custom decks you create
  • A word-by-word recap with the option to share your score
  • English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese

Play safely

headsup is played by holding your phone at your forehead and tilting it, sometimes while friends act things out. Please play sensibly:

  • Leave enough room to move, and be aware of the people and objects around you.
  • Don’t play near hazards such as stairs, roads, water, hot surfaces or traffic, and never while driving or walking somewhere you need to watch where you’re going.
  • Hold your phone securely so it doesn’t slip or hit anyone.
  • Children should play with adult supervision where appropriate.
