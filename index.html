Good Company: Tilt & Guess is a guessing game you play together, in person. Hold your iPhone to your forehead, your friends give clues, and you tilt to answer.

Download on the App StoreHow to play

For iPhone, iOS 17 or later. One purchase — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads.

How to play

Phone up. Fun starts.

1

Hold it up

Hold your iPhone sideways at your forehead so your friends can see the word.

2

Listen for clues

Friends describe, act and hint. You guess before the clock runs out.

3

Tilt to answer

Tilt down when you get it right, tilt up to skip. Prefer tapping? Play with on-screen buttons.

8 decks, 320 cards

Your own decks

Make a deck from your own word list — inside jokes welcome. It stays on your iPhone.

English and Chinese

English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Follows your device language, or switch in Settings.

Pauses for you

Leave the app mid-round and the game pauses automatically. Resume or finish when you’re back.

Sound and haptics

Feel each answer land. Turn sound or haptics off anytime in Settings.

Nothing collected

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Works fully offline.
