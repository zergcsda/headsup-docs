Privacy Policy

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Good Company does not have accounts, does not connect to the internet, does not run servers for your data, does not include advertising, analytics or other third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, Good Company stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app’s private storage (Application Support):

  • Custom decks you create
  • Your settings, such as language, round length, sound and haptics
  • Your last score

We cannot see this information. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on; those backups are managed by you and Apple. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Motion sensors

During a round, Good Company reads your device’s motion sensors (Core Motion gravity data) to tell when you tilt the phone down for a correct answer or up to skip. Motion data is used only while a round is in progress, is not stored, and never leaves your device. iOS does not ask for permission for this, and you can always play with on-screen buttons instead.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share after a round, Good Company opens the standard iOS share sheet with your score. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it, and we do not receive it.

Purchases

Good Company is a paid download from the App Store. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

Children

Good Company is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not collect personal information from children. The app has no ads and no links to external content during play.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Because your data stays on your iPhone, you are always in control of it:

  • Access and edit: view and change your decks and settings in the app.
  • Delete: delete a custom deck in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • No selling or sharing: we do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
